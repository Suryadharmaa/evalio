## Why pending is acceptable

The Evalio standard is:

```text
verified institutional logo
OR
Evalio fallback
```

not:

```text
whatever logo can be found fastest
```

This prevents athletics marks, outdated identities, sub-brand marks, or login-page downloads from becoming production assets.

## Trademark note

Copyright status and trademark status are separate. A simple text-logo may be public-domain for copyright purposes while remaining a protected university trademark.
