## Why pending is acceptable

The Evalio rule is:

```text
verified institutional logo
OR
Evalio fallback
```

not:

```text
fastest available logo regardless of accuracy
```

This prevents wrong campuses, athletics marks, outdated identities, and login-page downloads from becoming production assets.
